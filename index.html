Import Trades
A Holdings / Portfolio CSV (Name, Quantity, Avg Price, Last Traded, …) is also detected automatically and imported as open positions.
Broker tradebook (.xlsx or .csv) — the header row is found automatically even when the sheet has client-info rows above it, and buy/sell legs are matched FIFO into round trips. A tradewise exits report (entry/exit date, quantity, buy & sell value) also works. A broker Stocks P&L Statement (.xlsx with "Trade Level" & "Scrip Level" sheets) is detected automatically — realised trades, still-open holdings and the charges breakup in one go. Any other broker: columns Ticker, Entry Date, Entry Price, Exit Date, Exit Price, Qty (Direction optional).
